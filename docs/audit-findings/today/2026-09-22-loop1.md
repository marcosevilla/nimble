### P1-1 ReminderCatchUp performs urgency: "needs attention" copy and a red alert on the morning surface
- Anchor: `apps/desktop/src/components/today/ReminderCatchUp.tsx:34` (`role="alert" … text-destructive`), `:36` (`'This reminder time needs attention. Open the task to choose a valid time.'`), `:33` (section title "Reminders to review")
- Rubric: §1.1, §3.1 ("anything that performs urgency at the user"); §1.4 (color only for semantic meaning)
- Screens: light `today-review-light.png`, dark `today-review-dark.png` (second row copy)
- What's wrong: The strip is, functionally, the "you've been away" surface the rubric bans: it lists reminders that fired while the app was closed, tells the user one "needs attention", and on any refresh failure paints a red alert every 15 s (`:22`). Measured `text-destructive` on background is 4.70:1 light / 4.18:1 dark, the highest-chroma text on the page. The prior loop already fixed identical copy ("N items need attention") and identical red chrome on the calendar rail.
- Fix: Reword to neutral pull framing — "Reminders" + "No valid time yet — open the task to pick one"; render the refresh error as `text-muted-foreground` without `role="alert"` (the rows themselves are the signal). Not CROSS-SURFACE (strip renders only on Today).

### P1-3 The review wraps one real decision in two confirmation clicks, and the finish button lands below the fold
